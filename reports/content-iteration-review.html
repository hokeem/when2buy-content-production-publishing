Image production rule for the next iteration.

Every image must preserve the same visual subject and factual payload as the source: company/product/building/person/chart plus the decisive number or event. Generate a new, original entity-based scene in when2buy’s black/white/red system; add the official logo once, with clear space. Reject any pure-text card unless the source itself has no meaningful visual subject and the event is intrinsically text-led (for example, a service outage).
