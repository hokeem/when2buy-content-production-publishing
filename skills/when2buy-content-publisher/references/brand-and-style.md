## Visual grammar

- Use a square 1:1 canvas for the default X image.
- Use a black or near-black background, bold white typography, and controlled red accents. Use green only for genuinely positive/upward meaning.
- Make one number or phrase dominant enough to understand in two seconds on a phone.
- Use one supporting visual: company building, chip, market chart, calendar, filing, or transaction diagram.
- Keep visible copy short. Prefer a headline plus one supporting label; never place the full post on the image.
- Preserve strong alignment, generous empty space, sharp contrast, and a premium financial-news look.
- Proofread every number, ticker, currency sign, date, and company name after rendering.

## Text voice

Write native English for X. Sound like a fast, opinionated market observer, not a press release and not an AI explainer.

Default structure:

```text
[Company/ticker] just [event] [dominant number].

[Second decisive fact or contrast].

[One sharp investor implication.]
```

Preferred traits:

- 35-80 words, usually three short blocks.
- Lead with the event and number; do not open with background.
- Use plain verbs: `just filed`, `just priced`, `is buying`, `is cutting`, `is betting`.
- Use one opinionated implication, not a paragraph of balanced analysis.
- Use `$TICKER` when natural.
- Use emoji rarely and only when it improves tone; do not force slang.

Avoid:

- `This is when2buy, your AI stock friend` as a fixed ending.
- `In a significant development`, `It is worth noting`, and other corporate filler.
- long explanations, fake certainty, return promises, hashtag piles, or multiple conclusions.
- lightly paraphrasing a benchmark's distinctive hook or top reply.

## Historical text examples

Breaking financing:

```text
Intel just upsized its stock sale to $20B, priced at $95 per share.

That is a brutal reminder of how capital-intensive the AI and chip race has become.

Big ambition needs even bigger funding.
```

Business-model shift:

```text
Riot just landed a $9.1B, 20-year AI compute deal with Anthropic.

A former bitcoin mining story is turning into an AI infrastructure story.

That shift is worth watching.
```

Macro calendar:

```text
This week is basically an inflation stress test.

Wed: CPI
Thu: PPI + Jobless Claims
Fri: Retail Sales + Michigan Sentiment

Cooler inflation gives stocks room. Sticky prices kill the rate-cut party fast.
```

These examples demonstrate rhythm and structure only. Reverify every historical fact before reuse.
